# ISO/IEC 27002 — Information Security Controls

## What it is
Guidance standard providing implementation detail for the 93 controls in ISO/IEC 27001
Annex A. Not certifiable on its own.

## Current edition
**ISO/IEC 27002:2022** — third edition, February 2022

## Controls by theme

| Theme | Controls | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A.5 Organisational | 37 | Policies, roles, supplier security, threat intel |
| A.6 People | 8 | Screening, training, remote working |
| A.7 Physical | 14 | Perimeters, entry, clear desk, disposal |
| A.8 Technological | 34 | Endpoints, encryption, logging, SDLC |
