# Clause 9 — Performance Evaluation

## 9.1 Monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation
Determine what needs to be monitored and measured, methods, when, and who analyses results.
Retain documented information as evidence.

## 9.2 Internal audit
Conduct audits at planned intervals to determine whether the ISMS conforms to
requirements and is effectively implemented.

Audit programme must consider the importance of processes and results of previous audits.

## 9.3 Management review
Top management must review the ISMS at planned intervals.

**Inputs must include:** status of previous actions, changes in context and needs,
security performance (nonconformities, monitoring results, audit findings, objectives),
feedback from interested parties, risk assessment results, opportunities for improvement.

**Output:** decisions on improvement opportunities and any changes needed.
