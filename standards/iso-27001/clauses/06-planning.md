# Clause 6 — Planning

## 6.1 Actions to address risks and opportunities

### 6.1.1 General
Determine risks and opportunities to: ensure ISMS achieves intended outcomes,
prevent or reduce undesired effects, achieve continual improvement.

### 6.1.2 Information security risk assessment
Define and apply a risk assessment process that:
- Establishes risk acceptance criteria
- Identifies risks associated with loss of CIA (confidentiality, integrity, availability)
- Analyses and evaluates risks
- Produces comparable and reproducible results

### 6.1.3 Information security risk treatment
- Select appropriate risk treatment options
- Determine controls needed (compare against Annex A to check nothing is missed)
- Produce a Statement of Applicability (SoA) — this is a mandatory output
- Produce a risk treatment plan

**Key output:** Statement of Applicability (SoA)

## 6.2 Information security objectives and planning to achieve them
Set objectives that are measurable, monitored, communicated, and updated as needed.

## 6.3 Planning of changes
Changes to the ISMS must be carried out in a planned manner.
