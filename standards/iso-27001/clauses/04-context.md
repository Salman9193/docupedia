# Clause 4 — Context of the Organisation

## 4.1 Understanding the organisation and its context
Determine internal and external issues relevant to the organisation's purpose that
affect the ability to achieve the intended outcomes of the ISMS.

**Amd 1:2024 addition:** also determine whether climate change is a relevant issue.

*What to document:* A context analysis (PESTLE, SWOT, or similar). List of internal
and external issues. Climate change determination.

## 4.2 Understanding the needs and expectations of interested parties
Identify interested parties and their requirements relevant to information security.

**Amd 1:2024 note:** interested parties can have climate-related requirements.

*What to document:* Interested parties register with their relevant requirements.

## 4.3 Determining the scope of the ISMS
Define the boundaries and applicability of the ISMS.

*What to document:* Scope statement.

## 4.4 Information security management system
Establish, implement, maintain and continually improve the ISMS.
