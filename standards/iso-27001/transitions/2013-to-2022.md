# Transition: ISO/IEC 27001:2013 → 2022

## What changed structurally
- Annex A restructured from **14 clauses, 114 controls** → **4 themes, 93 controls**
- 11 new controls added
- 24 controls created by merging earlier controls
- 58 controls carried over (updated)
- Aligned with ISO harmonised structure (Annex SL)
