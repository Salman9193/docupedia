# ISO/IEC 27005 — Information Security Risk Management

## What it is
Guidance on implementing the risk management process required by ISO/IEC 27001
Clause 6.1. Not certifiable. Methodology-agnostic.

## Current edition
**ISO/IEC 27005:2022** — fourth edition

## Key process steps
1. Context establishment
2. Risk assessment (identification → analysis → evaluation)
3. Risk treatment
4. Risk acceptance
5. Risk communication and consultation
6. Risk monitoring and review
