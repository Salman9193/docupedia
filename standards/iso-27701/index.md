# ISO/IEC 27701 — Privacy Information Management Systems

## What it is
Certifiable standard for establishing and improving a Privacy Information Management
System (PIMS). Covers both PII Controllers and PII Processors.

## Current edition
**ISO/IEC 27701:2025** — second edition, October 2025

## Major change in 2025
Now a **standalone standard** — no longer requires ISO 27001 as a prerequisite.
Independently certifiable.

## Scope
Any organisation that collects, processes, stores or controls PII. Supports
compliance with GDPR, DPDP Act 2023 (India), CCPA, LGPD, PIPL.
