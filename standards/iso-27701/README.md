# ISO/IEC 27701 — Privacy Information Management Systems (PIMS)

## What it is
Certifiable standard for establishing, implementing, maintaining and improving a
Privacy Information Management System. Covers both PII Controllers and PII Processors.

## Current edition
**ISO/IEC 27701:2025** — second edition, published October 2025

## Major change in 2025
The 2019 edition was an *extension* to ISO 27001 — you could only certify it on top
of an existing ISMS certification. The 2025 edition is a **standalone standard**,
independently certifiable. No ISO 27001 prerequisite.

ISO/IEC 27706:2025 now specifies requirements for certification bodies auditing PIMS,
replacing reliance on ISO/IEC TS 27006-2.

## Scope
Any organisation that collects, processes, stores or controls PII. Supports
compliance with GDPR, DPDP Act 2023 (India), CCPA, LGPD, PIPL.

## Structure (2025 edition)
- Clauses 4–10 mirror ISO management system structure, tailored for privacy
- Annex A: controls for PII Controllers and Processors (consolidated into A.1, A.2, A.3)
- Expanded scope: biometric data, health data, IoT, AI-related privacy risks
